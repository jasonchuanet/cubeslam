Privacy on this Cube Slam server

This self-hosted version includes no analytics, remote fonts, or cloud backend. The server holds live room membership and relays connection setup messages while you play. Empty rooms are removed when players disconnect, and restarting the server clears all room state.

If you allow camera and microphone access, video, audio, and game data travel over an encrypted WebRTC connection to the other player. A host-configured TURN relay may carry that encrypted traffic when a direct connection is unavailable. The other player may learn your network address as part of WebRTC connection setup.

Game preferences are stored locally in your browser. Social share buttons open the service you select. Your hosting operator may apply additional logging, retention, and privacy policies.
